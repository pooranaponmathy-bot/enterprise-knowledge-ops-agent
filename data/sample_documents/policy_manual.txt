Policy Manual

1. Compliance Oversight
All employees must report material compliance incidents to the Legal and Compliance Office within 24 hours. Major incidents include regulatory violations, data breaches, or repeated policy non-compliance.

2. Escalation Policy
Escalation should move from team leads to Division Compliance, and then to General Counsel when the incident is high impact or threatens legal exposure.

3. Exception Handling
Any policy deviation must be approved through a written exception request and managed by Risk and Compliance.
